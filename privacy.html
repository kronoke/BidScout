Information BidScout currently uses

The current site can process search terms and locations you enter in the browser to re-rank opportunity results. Public-source data

Business and government opportunity records displayed by BidScout may come from public websites, government databases, organizational announcements, or other publicly accessible sources. Source links are provided where available.

The hosting platform may use technical cookies or analytics technologies for security, performance, and traffic measurement. BidScout does not currently sell personal information.

Opportunity records may be retained for research, deduplication, and feed-quality purposes. Search inputs entered into the current browser-based matcher are not intended to create a persistent customer profile unless a future account feature explicitly says otherwise.

Contact and changes

This policy may be updated as BidScout adds accounts, alerts, billing, or additional data services. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
