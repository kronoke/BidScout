ABOUT BIDSCOUT

Opportunity research without the noise.

BidScout helps small businesses find and evaluate public opportunities that are easy to miss when information is spread across government systems, business announcements, permits, project pages, and other public sources.

What BidScout does

BidScout organizes two kinds of opportunity intelligence: formal government solicitations and private-market business signals. It scores and summarizes records to help users decide what deserves deeper research.

What BidScout does not do

BidScout does not guarantee contract awards, purchasing intent, eligibility, revenue, or the accuracy of third-party source material. Original source documents remain the source of truth.

Why it exists

Small businesses often do not have dedicated procurement or sales-research teams. BidScout is designed to reduce the time required to find promising opportunities and make the first screening decision.
